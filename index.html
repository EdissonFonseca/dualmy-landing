A cycle that doesn't stay on paper

Dualmy doesn't hand you a report and leave. It goes with you from the moment you describe the problem until the improvement is working, and then it looks for the next one.

  1. 1

    Understand

    You tell us what your company does and how it does it, calmly and without endless forms.

  2. 2

    Discover

    You describe a challenge and we work out together what causes it and what it costs.

  3. 3

    Recommend

    We propose a solution using what Dualmy can really do. If something can't be done, we tell you.

  4. 4

    Build

    We put the solution together with you: you review it, test it and switch it on when you're ready.

  5. 5

    Operate

    The solution works with your data and your people, and you see what happens and what it costs.

  6. 6

    Improve

    What we observe reveals new opportunities. And the cycle starts again.

Talk it through, or do it yourself. You end up in the same place.

Everything Dualmy can do in conversation, you can also do with visual tools. And the other way around. Use whichever path feels more comfortable on any given day.

By conversation

    Dualmy suggests and prepares, but publishing or switching something on is always a person's decision.

    Actions that can't be reversed stop until someone approves them.

  • What's yours stays in your business

    Each company is kept separate from the others. People's data is looked after and you can delete it.
